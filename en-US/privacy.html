Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026

Snack Attack is an offline single-player game. The developer is Vinicius de Carvalho Zanini.

Game data

The game does not collect or transmit personal data. There is no registration, login, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

What stays on your device

Length records, statistics and preferences for music, effects, haptics and the tutorial are saved locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer. The game does not synchronize records between devices.

Deleting local data

Deleting the app removes its local storage. System backups may follow your device and iCloud settings; Apple and you manage those backups.

Contacting support

If you email vdcz@proton.me, the developer receives your address and the content you choose to share. This information is used to respond to and resolve your request, and is kept for as long as needed for support or applicable obligations. You may request deletion through the same email address. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

External services

The App Store and TestFlight are Apple services governed by Apple policies. You may choose to share diagnostics or feedback with Apple. Support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical access logs under its own policy. This is separate from the offline game.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the game or its data practices change. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to vdcz@proton.me.
